# Research sources

This folder preserves the enacted texts against which the Illinois officer
certification draft is written.

## Controlling source

### Illinois SB 315 / Public Act 104-0538

- [`illinois_sb0315_pa_104_0538_enrolled.pdf`](./illinois_sb0315_pa_104_0538_enrolled.pdf)
- [`illinois_sb0315_pa_104_0538_enrolled.txt`](./illinois_sb0315_pa_104_0538_enrolled.txt)
- Official source: <https://www.ilga.gov/documents/legislation/104/SB/PDF/10400SB0315lv.pdf>

This is the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act that the proposed bill
would amend. The PDF is the official enrolled bill publication. The plain-text
file is a convenience extraction and may not preserve underlining,
strikethrough, spacing, pagination, or other legislative typography. The
official publication controls.

## Enacted sibling statutes

### New York S.8828 / Chapter 96 of the Laws of 2026

- [`new_york_raise_act_s8828_chapter_96_2026.pdf`](./new_york_raise_act_s8828_chapter_96_2026.pdf)
- [`new_york_raise_act_s8828_chapter_96_2026.txt`](./new_york_raise_act_s8828_chapter_96_2026.txt)
- Official source: <https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S8828>

The enacted New York RAISE Act uses the same frontier-model threshold,
framework, transparency, incident-reporting, disclosure-registration, and
entity-penalty family. It is included for comparison because the first officer
certification research draft was written as an amendment to this statute.

### California SB 53 / Chapter 138 of the Statutes of 2025

- [`california_sb53_chapter_138_2025.txt`](./california_sb53_chapter_138_2025.txt)
- Official source: <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB53>

California's Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act is the
earliest member of the enacted California-New York-Illinois statutory family.
The plain-text file is a convenience conversion of the official chaptered
bill page. The official publication controls.

## Relevance to the proposed amendment

The proposed bill does not import new technical standards from these sources.
It adds a natural-person signature and enforcement layer to duties Illinois has
already enacted. The sibling statutes are preserved to identify shared
language, test cross-state consistency, and prevent the proposal from claiming
an innovation where the underlying duty is already law.
