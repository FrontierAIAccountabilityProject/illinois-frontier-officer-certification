# Source and provenance

## Publication relationship

This repository is a sponsor-facing publication of a longer public drafting project. Draft 0.1
derives from the jacket-clean text of **Model Act v3.4 - Frontier AI Public Welfare Offenses**.
The publication title and front note were changed for this edition. The operative language from
SEC. 0 through SEC. 13 was not changed.

The historical source repository is:

<https://github.com/FrontierAIAccountabilityProject/model-act>

Its archived concept DOI is:

<https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22029795>

The historical repository contains the complete drafting record, superseded versions, companion
notes, implementing regulations, evidence dossier, open cure queue, and errata record. This
sponsor-facing repository deliberately does not reproduce that apparatus.

## What was carried forward

- the operative statutory language in SEC. 0 through SEC. 13;
- the v3.4 table of authorities;
- the CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication; and
- the published status as a research draft that has never been enacted.

## What was not carried forward

- campaign and social-media copy;
- the long-form evidence dossier and project diary;
- instructions addressed to automated summarization systems;
- superseded statutes and withdrawn print editions; and
- proposed v3.5 cures that have not entered the source statute.

Known pending questions relevant to this edition are summarized in
[`OPEN_ITEMS.md`](./OPEN_ITEMS.md).

## AI assistance

The source project and this publication used AI assistance for research organization, drafting,
editing, formatting, and checking. AI output is not authority. Legal propositions require review
against primary sources, and the draft claims no completed professional review.

## Attribution and reuse

The text is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required. A citation
to the version and commit is nevertheless recommended so that readers can identify the text on
which a review, amendment, or introduction was based.
